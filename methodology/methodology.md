# Methodology Overview

The process used to conduct this audit was for the purpose of investigating, diagnosing, and correcting the security posture of the NuCore environment.

1. Take inventory of the identities, accounts, resources, and business assets present within the cloud.
2. Map the relationships between various resources to see who or what has access to business critical assets.
3. Audit the most at-risk areas including the origin point of the environments vulnerability.
4. Recommend or make high-impact, low-effort fixes to the top 3 priority issues.
5. Document findings and score the sections under review according to their risk level.

## Summary

This methodology is meant for broadly understanding the current security posture and stability of the environment. 

information retrieval and gaining visibility into the environment is the initial step. Then finding the relationships shared between users, accounts, and startup resources.
