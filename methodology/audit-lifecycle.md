# AUDIT LIFECYCLE

## 1. Inventory

Identify and document cloud resources, identities, applications, storage locations, network components, and monitoring systems within scope.

### Objective

Establish visibility into the environment before assessing risk.

***

## 2. Relationship Mapping

Map trust relationships, access paths, service dependencies, and data flow between resources.

### Objective

Understand how compromise or misconfiguration in one area could impact other systems.

***

## 3. Risk-Focused Audit

Prioritize review of the most exposed or operationally critical areas, including IAM, compute, storage, and logging configurations.

### Objective

Identify high-impact weaknesses with realistic exploitation potential.

***

## 4. Low-Effort Remediation

Implement or recommend practical fixes that significantly reduce risk without requiring major architectural redesign.

### Objective

Improve security posture quickly while minimizing operational disruption.

***

## 5. Documentation & Scoring

Document findings, remediation recommendations, and overall environment risk posture using the defined scoring methodology.

### Objective

Create a repeatable baseline for future audits and continuous improvement.
