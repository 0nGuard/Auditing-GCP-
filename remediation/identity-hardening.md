# IAM HARDENING

- Stop broad permissions and allow users to only have access to the resources needed to complete the task. And apply JIT (just-in-time) access to manage availability of resources during a specified time.
- Utilize segmentation and maintain separation between service accounts, principals or business critical assets such as data buckets or virtual machines.
- Disable accounts and delete long-standing keys that are not in use. Do not leave them unattended.
- Activate usage logs for better visibility of identities and their activity.
- Create custom roles with pre-defined privileges and use workload and/or workforce identity federation.
