# Backup Findings

Backup and Recovery is the preservation layer of the environment. No indication of backup resources or disaster recovery plan.

## Findings

### Backup - 01 - Non - existent backup resources and recovery policy

- Backups for business critical assets and data are not indicated.
- No policies or a plan for post - incident recovery.

## Risk Impact

Without a backup system and visible recovery preparedness the business is vulnerable to data loss/corruption. 
This  leads to regulatory violation, demonstrates low-compliance standards, and can cause reputational damage.
